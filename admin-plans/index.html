Important Notes

• Plan prices and limits are saved under systemSettings/planConfiguration.
• Owner subscriptions are read from the existing users/{uid} documents.
• This page does not automatically activate paid plans, process payments, or extend subscription dates.
• Enforcing plan limits in the actual app pages must be implemented separately. Client-side plan checks alone are not secure access control.
• Protect admin-only writes using Firestore Rules and, for stronger security, a trusted backend or Firebase Authentication custom claims.
